Investment & Economics Glossary

Search common investing and economics terms.

P/E ratio

Share price divided by earnings per share. A low value does not automatically mean undervaluation.

P/B ratio

Share price divided by book value per share.

ROE

Net income divided by shareholders’ equity; measures profitability relative to equity.

ROA

Profitability relative to total assets; definitions vary.

EPS

Earnings per share: net income attributable to common shareholders divided by weighted-average shares.

Free cash flow

Cash remaining after capital expenditures; definitions vary.

Margin of safety

The gap between conservative estimated intrinsic value and market price.

Intrinsic value

An estimate of a business’s economic worth based on future benefits and risks.

MBO

Management buyout: acquisition led by company management.

ETF

Exchange-traded fund: a fund traded on an exchange.

Inflation

A sustained increase in the general price level.

Screening

Filtering securities using specified financial or other criteria.
