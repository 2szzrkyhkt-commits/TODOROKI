Investment & Economics Glossary

Search common investing and economics terms.

P/E ratio

Share price divided by earnings per share. A low value does not automatically mean undervaluation.

P/B ratio

Share price divided by book value per share.

ROE

Net income divided by shareholders’ equity; measures profitability relative to equity.

ROA

Profitability relative to total assets; definitions vary.

EPS

Earnings per share: net income attributable to common shareholders divided by weighted-average shares.

Free cash flow

Cash remaining after capital expenditures; definitions vary.

Margin of safety

The gap between conservative estimated intrinsic value and market price.

Intrinsic value

An estimate of a business’s economic worth based on future benefits and risks.

MBO

Management buyout: acquisition led by company management.

ETF

Exchange-traded fund: a fund traded on an exchange.

Inflation

A sustained increase in the general price level.

Screening

Filtering securities using specified financial or other criteria.

Operating cash flow

Cash generated or used by a company's core operating activities.

Financial cash flow

Cash movements from borrowing, repaying debt, issuing shares and distributing funds to shareholders.

Current ratio

Current assets divided by current liabilities; a short-term liquidity measure.

Equity ratio

Shareholders' equity divided by total assets; a measure of financial structure.

Dividend yield

Annual dividend per share divided by the share price; not a guaranteed return.

Payout ratio

Dividends divided by earnings, typically expressed as a percentage.

Debt-to-equity ratio

Interest-bearing debt relative to shareholders' equity; definitions may differ.

TOB / Tender offer

A public offer to purchase shares under specified terms and during a stated period.

Share buyback

A company's repurchase of its own shares; effects depend on price and financing.

Capital allocation

Management's decisions on using cash for operations, investment, acquisitions or shareholder returns.

Real interest rate

An interest rate adjusted for inflation; approximately nominal rate minus inflation.

Compound interest

Growth resulting when accumulated returns also earn returns; losses can compound too.

Market capitalization

Share price multiplied by shares outstanding, with share-count definitions varying.

Enterprise value

A valuation measure often calculated as market capitalization plus debt minus cash, subject to adjustments.
