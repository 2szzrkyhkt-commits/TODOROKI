Economics: Prices, Interest Rates and Daily Life

Economics becomes clearer when we examine purchasing power, interest rates and who bears the costs of change.

Purchasing power, not just nominal amounts

A higher salary does not always mean a higher standard of living if prices rise faster. Compare changes in nominal income with inflation and taxes.

Interest rates and the flow of money

Interest rates affect borrowing, savings, investment and asset prices. The impact differs for borrowers, lenders, households and businesses.

Different perspectives and time horizons

Separate immediate price movements from long-term structural changes. Consider households, firms and governments independently.
