Business and Management: How Companies Allocate Resources

Understanding how a business uses people, capital and time can be more informative than focusing only on reported profits.

Identify the competitive advantage

Ask what customers value, how the company differentiates itself and whether its advantage can last.

Follow capital allocation

Examine spending on operations, research, acquisitions, dividends and share buybacks. Assess whether those decisions create lasting value.

Compare plans with results

Management promises should be tested against cash flow, profitability and outcomes over several years.
